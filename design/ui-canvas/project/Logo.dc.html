Use it well

  • Keep clear space of at least the stem width on every side.
  • The main line is always drawn on top of the branch.
  • Wordmark: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next Bold, SIL OFL 1.1. Convert to outlines for print.
  • Do not rotate, add effects, or recolor the lines with other branch colors.
